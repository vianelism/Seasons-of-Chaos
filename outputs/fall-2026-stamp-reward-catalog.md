# Seasons of Chaos — Fall 2026 Stamp & Reward Catalog

**Season:** September 1–November 30, 2026  
**Theme:** Cozy fall, Halloween chaos, and Friendsgiving  
**Structure:** 18 seasonal stamps: 15 public and 3 secret  
**Philosophy:** No leaderboard, no required participation, no reward requires every stamp.

## Member-facing season description

Fall 2026 is three months of cozy nonsense, spooky nonsense, and well-fed nonsense. Join whatever sounds fun, skip whatever does not, and collect completely prestigious passport stamps along the way. Every stamp stays in your lifetime Seasons of Chaos passport.

## Reward ladder

Secret stamps count toward reward totals, but members never need a secret stamp to reach a reward. Rewards should be granted quietly or celebrated according to the member's preference.

| Threshold | Reward | Slug | Type | Fulfillment |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 3 Fall stamps | 🍁 **First Leaves** | `first-leaves` | Recognition | Bot unlock message and optional temporary `🍁 First Leaves` Discord role. |
| 6 Fall stamps | 🧣 **Certified Cozy** | `certified-cozy` | Discord role | Seasonal role with a warm fall color; no extra permissions. |
| 10 Fall stamps | 🎤 **Fall Main Character** | `fall-main-character` | Community perk | Member may choose or submit one upcoming This-or-That question or seasonal prompt. Moderator schedules it. |
| 14 Fall stamps | 👑 **Fall Chaos Legend** | `fall-chaos-legend` | Recognition + role | Special seasonal role and inclusion in the end-of-season celebration. Do not require all 18 stamps. |

## Automation model

Fall 2026 is automation-first. The bot scans configured channels every five minutes and awards objective participation, photo, movie-night, and game-night stamps. Members use `/check-in` for activities that cannot be safely inferred from a Discord message. Secret activity-day achievements and reward thresholds unlock automatically. `/award` and `/revoke` remain moderator fallback tools.

## Moderator rules

1. Award for participation, creativity, connection, or delight—not volume, popularity, spending, or polished presentation.
2. A member can earn each Fall 2026 stamp once.
3. Do not require photos of faces, children, homes, purchases, meals, costumes, or private life. Alternatives always count.
4. Never use a secret stamp to mock, expose, or embarrass someone.
5. If an activity overlaps two stamps, both may be awarded when both requirements are genuinely met.
6. Do not announce a stamp publicly when the member prefers a private response.
7. No leaderboard and no public ranking by stamp count.
8. Fall rewards stop at 14 of 18 so members can skip activities and still reach the final recognition tier.

## Recommended implementation changes

- Keep the 8 existing Fall mappings.
- Add the 10 new stamp definitions in a new D1 migration.
- Replace the current single Fall reward with the four-tier reward ladder above.
- Keep global stamps unmapped from a specific season.
- Add optional `role_reward_id` values only after the Discord roles are created.
- Treat **Fall Main Character** as a moderator-fulfilled perk rather than an automatic Discord role.
